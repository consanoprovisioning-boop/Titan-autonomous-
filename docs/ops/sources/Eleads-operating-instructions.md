# Customer Traffic and Appointment Growth — operating instructions

Filed from Glenn Bordine’s Eleads customer-contact workflow packet for Valdosta Nissan and Valdosta Mitsubishi. This is the source packet for `docs/ops/eleads-valdosta.md`.

## Primary project goal

Increase qualified dealership foot traffic and confirmed sales, upgrade, and complimentary vehicle-appraisal appointments for Bordine, Glenn through timely, personalized, consent-aware customer follow-up that is fully documented and verifiable in Eleads.

## What success means

Customer outcomes:

- More specific appointment commitments with an agreed day and time.
- More customers arriving for appraisals, upgrades, and vehicle-selection visits.
- Fast, helpful responses to new leads and direct customer replies.

Operational outcomes:

- Every send and completed task is visible in CRM history.
- Email and SMS are counted as separate activities.
- Management reports match the actual work performed.

## Authorized identity and rooftop scope

- All actions must be attributed to Bordine, Glenn.
- Work only in Valdosta Nissan (28206) and Valdosta Mitsubishi (28546).
- Do not work in The TKO Autogroup (6220).
- Verify both the selected rooftop and user identity before any CRM action.

## Build the queue

1. Select and verify the correct rooftop.
2. Open the Daily Organizer with status Open and contact type All.
3. Review Glenn-assigned work first, then store-wide opportunities when authorized.
4. Capture customer, due time, task type, vehicle, assigned consultant, comments, and opportunity link.
5. Deduplicate multiple tasks tied to the same customer and opportunity.

## Priority order

| Rank | Opportunity type | Primary objective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Direct replies and explicit interest | Answer the customer, then secure a day and time. |
| 2 | New leads | Prepare email and eligible SMS within the four-minute operating target while the active session is available. |
| 3 | Trade, appraisal, lease maturity, missed appointment, recent inquiry | Offer a complimentary review and two future appointment choices. |
| 4 | Qualified dormant new/used vehicle leads | Re-engage after full history and permission review. |
| 5 | Past owners and equity opportunities | Discuss current ownership, appraisal, and possible upgrade needs. |
| 6 | Birthday and vehicle anniversary | Relationship follow-up with a clear appraisal or upgrade invitation. |

## Ownership-timing rule

Use 12 months as the current past-purchase screening guideline. A recent inquiry overrides the last-purchase timing screen and should be reviewed on its own current intent. Model year alone never proves ownership duration or eligibility.

## Profile inspection before contact

No profile qualifies from the list view alone. Open the customer and opportunity and allow the full activity feed to load.

Review communication history, customer status, vehicle and transaction facts, and permissions and validity as listed in `docs/ops/eleads-valdosta.md`.

## 72-hour qualification rule

For another salesperson’s potential customer, any qualifying communication in the preceding 72 hours holds the profile from cross-consultant outreach. Qualifying activity includes a contact note, sent or received SMS, inbound or outbound call correspondence, customer email response, or actively managed appointment. Use the exact timestamp, not only the task comment.

Glenn’s own customers: prepare follow-up only after checking the most recent email/SMS timing and all permissions.

If history is unavailable, mark Manual verification required; do not assume the customer is untouched.

## Outreach

- Answer the customer’s latest question or concern first.
- Use verified customer, vehicle, trade, ownership, and timing details.
- Keep email generally under 120 words; keep SMS concise and conversational.
- Make one clear appointment ask and offer two specific future choices.
- Focus on a complimentary appraisal, ownership review, upgrade exploration, or vehicle-selection visit.
- If the original vehicle is no longer the best fit, accurately offer verified alternatives without creating false availability.
- Vehicle-specific secondary line: “all possible qualifying incentives, rebates, and special manufacturer APR programs.”
- Never promise eligibility, approval, a payment, an appraisal value, a specific savings amount, an APR, a rebate, or vehicle availability unless it was verified live and stated with required qualifications.

## Channel and timing

- Email: valid permitted email, no opt-out/hard bounce, approved draft.
- SMS: valid SMS-permitted phone, no DNT/Stop, approved draft, 9:00 AM–7:00 PM America/New_York.
- Phone: valid callable number and no do-not-call restriction. Autonomous calls require telephony integration. Record the actual call outcome.

## Safe-send sequence

1. Confirm rooftop, customer, sender, channel permission, recipient, subject, and message.
2. Obtain Glenn’s approval for the exact draft or batch.
3. Preview the message and confirm the final send control.
4. Click Send once.
5. Refresh/recheck activity history before any retry.
6. Verify the completed CRM entry under Bordine, Glenn.

Email and SMS are separate completed activities. Required notes: `Manual email completed via system.` and `SMS text completed via system.`

A draft, blank composer, preview, or processing screen is not proof of delivery.

## Appointments

A solid appointment requires a customer commitment to a specific day and time. Interest without an agreed time remains follow-up, not Appointment Set.

Pause and present the exact customer profile, rooftop, appointment date/time, vehicle or appraisal purpose, arrival notes, and proposed confirmation-call timing. Save only after approval.

Dealership task-scheduling hours: 9 AM–7 PM Monday–Friday; 9 AM–6 PM Saturday.

## Daily operating cycle

Verify → triage → inspect → prepare → approve every exact email and SMS → execute → convert day-and-time commitments → clean up unused tabs.

New-lead target: while the authenticated session is available, complete the approved first email and eligible SMS within four minutes. If login, browser control, approval timing, or consent review prevents the target, record the reason instead of claiming compliance.
